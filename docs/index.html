Split Me

Split. Settle. Done.

Split Me scans a receipt, works out who ordered what, and tells each person exactly what they owe — tax and service included. Everything happens on your phone.

Common questions

How does the scanning work? Is my receipt uploaded anywhere?

No. Receipts are read by Apple's on-device text recogniser. The photo is never uploaded and is not kept — only the items you confirm are saved. There is no AI service and no server involved.

It read an item wrong.

Every scan opens a review screen first, precisely because paper receipts are creased, faded and inconsistent. Fix any name or price there, untick anything that isn't an item, and add anything it missed, before it reaches the bill.

Why does it ask for my location?

Two separate, optional things. While using the app lets it suggest nearby restaurants when naming a bill. Always is only for the Essentials reminder — noticing you have left home requires iOS to check in the background. Refuse either and the app still works; the reminder falls back to a daily one at a time you choose.

Are the tax and service calculations right for restaurants here?

Yes — both conventions are supported. If menu prices exclude tax and service, they are added to the subtotal and split in proportion to what each person ordered. If prices already include them, nothing is added twice.

Do the shares always add up?

Always. Money is handled in whole piastres, and leftover pennies are handed out one at a time rather than rounded away, so the shares total the bill exactly.

Can I use it in Arabic?

Yes. Tap the globe in the top corner to switch between English and Egyptian Arabic, with full right-to-left layout. The receipt reader also handles Arabic receipts and Arabic-Indic numerals.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

The open endpoint of exchangerate-api.com, which updates daily. Rates are cached on your device with the date they were published, so the converter still works offline and always tells you how old its numbers are.
